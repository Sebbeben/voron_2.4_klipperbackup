## Notes
- Spools from 2019 (TPU #16, PA #17) and anything stored open: dry before use (`DRY_ABS`-style bed
  drying or a filament dryer). PA must be dried every time (4-6 h at 70-80 C) and printed from a dry box.
- PLA/PETG in an enclosed Voron: doors open / lid off, chamber below ~35 C, or heat creep in the
  Rapido.
- POM: "ultimate adhesion challenge" - needs a POM-specific bed surface (e.g. glue stick / Magigoo PP) and
  sticks to almost nothing; test small parts first.
- Per-brand Orca presets (temperature, flow ratio, pressure advance) are made once each brand has been
  calibrated on this printer (flow + PA + max volumetric flow tests after the A/B motor rebuild).
