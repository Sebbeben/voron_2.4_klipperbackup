2. INA3221 input filter resistor error (IN- sinks Vbus/670k extra)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Rfilt   10 ohm, Vbus   24 V: offset  358.2 uV =  35.8 mA @10m,  17.9 mA @20m,   7.2 mA @50m
Rfilt   10 ohm, Vbus    5 V: offset   74.6 uV =   7.5 mA @10m,   3.7 mA @20m,   1.5 mA @50m
Rfilt    1 ohm, Vbus   24 V: offset   35.8 uV =   3.6 mA @10m,   1.8 mA @20m,   0.7 mA @50m
Rfilt    1 ohm, Vbus    5 V: offset    7.5 uV =   0.7 mA @10m,   0.4 mA @20m,   0.1 mA @50m
Rfilt   10 ohm + 100 nF differential: fc =     80 kHz
Rfilt    1 ohm + 100 nF differential: fc =    796 kHz
-> 10 ohm gives up to 36 mA offset on the 10 mOhm channels (more than the INA's own
   80 uV max). 1 ohm keeps it at 3.6 mA, below the INA offset spec, and still filters
   the >1 MHz transients TI's 7.4.3 is about. Decision: R1-R18 = 1 ohm.

3. 24 V bus vs INA3221 26 V abs max
-----------------------------------
SMBJ24A starts conducting at 26.7 V (min) and clamps to 38.9 V at peak current:
it does NOT keep the INA below 26.0 V. It only stops big surges.
-> PSU must be set to 24.0 V; braking spikes are absorbed by the Spider bulk caps.
   If the bus ever needs headroom: INA238/INA228 (85 V).

4. Open-drain alert pull-ups (R19, R20 = 10k to 3V3)
----------------------------------------------------
Sink current 0.33 mA per line (INA spec VOL 0.4 V at 3 mA) -> OK, 4 sinkers wired-OR.
Rise time with ~100 pF cable: 2.2 us -> fine for an alarm line.

5. Fuse-blown LEDs (LED + R across each fuse; lights when the fuse is open AND a load is connected)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
24 V rail, R 10.0k: LED 2.21 mA, resistor  48.8 mW (0603 = 100 mW)
 5 V rail, R  1.0k: LED 3.10 mA, resistor   9.6 mW (0603 = 100 mW)
Leakage into a disconnected/off load with intact fuse: 0 (LED shorted by the fuse).

7. Current transformers (ZMCT103C, 1000:1)
------------------------------------------
Datasheet accuracy, phase and linearity are given at 50.0 ohm burden.
Old design used 100/150 ohm (outside the spec). New: 49.9 ohm 0.1 % on all four.
CT1 total           6.3 A ->   314 mV rms, clips at 22.7 A rms (sine), 1 LSB = 16.1 mA =  3.7 W
CT2 bed 750 W       3.3 A ->   165 mV rms, clips at 22.7 A rms (sine), 1 LSB = 16.1 mA =  3.7 W
CT3 chamber 500 W   2.2 A ->   110 mV rms, clips at 22.7 A rms (sine), 1 LSB = 16.1 mA =  3.7 W
CT4 spare           2.0 A ->   100 mV rms, clips at 22.7 A rms (sine), 1 LSB = 16.1 mA =  3.7 W
RMS over 800 samples/window: quantisation noise ~LSB/sqrt(12*N) -> < 1 mA. Resolution is not the limit;
CT accuracy (0.2 % class A / 0.4 % class E) and calibration are.
SMAJ5.0CA across the burden: conducts from 6.4 V = 128 mA secondary = 91 A rms primary -> only faults/inrush.

8. Mains voltage via 9 V AC adapter
-----------------------------------
R72 100k/10k (/11.0):  9.00 V rms -> 1.16 V peak at the ADC (limit 1.60 V) OK
R72 100k/10k (/11.0): 12.67 V rms -> 1.63 V peak at the ADC (limit 1.60 V) TOO CLOSE
R72 120k/10k (/13.0):  9.00 V rms -> 0.98 V peak at the ADC (limit 1.60 V) OK
R72 120k/10k (/13.0): 12.67 V rms -> 1.38 V peak at the ADC (limit 1.60 V) OK
-> R72 = 120k: 9 V nominal gives ~1.0 V peak, worst case ~1.38 V, 0.22 V margin.
Adapter loop current ~0.1 mA flows adapter -> R72 -> R73 -> VBIAS -> adapter (J21 sleeve = VBIAS):
it does not pass through the bias divider, so no 50 Hz ripple is injected into the CT channels.
Same for the CTs: each pair returns to VBIAS, burden current stays on the CT board.

9. ADC front end (1k + 10 nF C0G, BAV99 clamps)
-----------------------------------------------
fc = 15.9 kHz, phase at 50 Hz = 0.18 deg (identical on all channels -> cancels in V*I)
Charge sharing: 10 nF vs ~5 pF ADC sample cap -> 0.05 % kick, recovers via 1k in <1 us
Fault: CT TVS at 9.2 V -> BAV99 clamp current through 1k = 3.4 mA into +3V3_MCU (MCU pin < 5 mA) OK
RJ45 unplugged: CTx_S would float -> add 100k CTx_S -> VBIAS on the main board (reads 0 A).
  Load on the burden: 49.9 ohm || 100k = 0.05 % low -> calibrated out.

11. I2C
-------
Pi pull-ups 1.8k on GPIO2/3 -> R21/R22 DNP. 4 targets: 0x40, 0x41, 0x42 (INA), 0x50 (STM32 app),
0x51 (STM32 ROM bootloader, AN2606 Rev 62 table 91: I2C1 PB6/PB7, 0b1010001x). No collisions.
The ROM bootloader stretches SCL during erase/write; the Pi's hardware I2C handles long clock
stretching badly -> flash with the i2c-gpio overlay (bit-banged, supports stretching) at 100 kHz.
App firmware: answer from buffered registers, no stretching (I2C NOSTRETCH).
